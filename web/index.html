Debt financing: value (APV) & covenant check

Financing is valued as adjusted present value: the unlevered NPV above plus the present value of the interest tax shield, discounted at the loan rate. The term defaults to the decision's horizon; a longer term leaves a balance that is repaid as a balloon in the final modelled year.
