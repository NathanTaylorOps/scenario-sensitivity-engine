NPV range (P90 – P50 – P10)

P90 = conservative case (90% chance of doing at least this well), P50 = median, P10 = optimistic case (only a 10% chance of doing this well). Read left-to-right as worst-to-best, not low-to-high confidence.

Goal-seek: what would it take to break even?

Holds every other driver at its base case and solves for the value this one driver alone would need to reach for NPV to hit zero — the question a GM asks more often than "what's the NPV".
